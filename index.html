Who We Are

A Data Analytics And Operational Intelligence Company

Operationally critical organisations generate enormous volumes of data — from production and equipment through to safety, environment and agriculture. Very little of it reaches the people who need it, in a form they can act on, while it still matters.

Mokay Group closes that gap. We design, build and support the analytics platforms, dashboards and data pipelines that turn raw operational data into decisions — engineered for environments where uptime, safety and asset performance carry real consequences.

  • Specialists, Not Generalists

    We work where operational performance, asset management, safety and real-time data are critical — not across every sector.

  • Built Around Your Data

    We integrate what you already run — SCADA, IoT, ERP, field capture and spreadsheets — rather than replacing it.

  • Designed With The Users

    Systems are developed in consultation with the operational personnel who have to use them every shift.

What We Do

Seven Capabilities, One Objective

Everything we build serves the same end — turning operational data into decisions your organisation can act on and defend.

Data Analytics & Business Intelligence

Turning fragmented operational records into clear, trustworthy analysis for every level of the organisation.

  • Trend and variance analysis
  • Executive and operational reporting
  • Cost and productivity insight

Operational Intelligence

A live view of what is happening across the operation right now, not a report assembled weeks later.

  • Real-time operational visibility
  • Exception and threshold alerting
  • Shift and site comparison

Performance Monitoring & Reporting

Defined targets, measured consistently, reported automatically — so performance conversations start from facts.

  • KPI frameworks and scorecards
  • Automated scheduled reporting
  • Target versus actual tracking

Safety & Risk Analytics

Safety, environmental and compliance data captured as it happens and structured to withstand audit.

  • Incident and near-miss analysis
  • Control verification tracking
  • Audit-ready compliance records

Asset & Equipment Data Management

A reliable record of every asset — its condition, history, utilisation and the cost it carries.

  • Asset registers and histories
  • Utilisation and downtime analysis
  • Maintenance and failure indicators

Custom Digital Platforms & Dashboards

Purpose-built tools shaped around your workflow, your terminology and your reporting lines.

  • Role-based dashboards
  • Field and mobile data capture
  • Bespoke operational platforms

Data Integration & Automation

Connecting the systems you already run so data flows once, cleanly, without manual re-keying.

  • SCADA, IoT and ERP integration
  • Automated data pipelines
  • Validation and quality controls
