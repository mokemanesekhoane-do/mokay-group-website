Where We Specialise

Six Sectors Where Operational Data Carries Real Consequence

We do not serve every industry. We work where asset performance, safety, productivity and real-time data have direct commercial and human impact — and where the cost of deciding late is measurable.

01 Mining & Resources 02 Industrial & Manufacturing 03 Energy & Utilities 04 Infrastructure & Construction 05 Agriculture 06 Health
01 Extraction & processing

Mining & Resources

From exploration drilling through extraction, processing and rehabilitation, mining generates data continuously and consumes it slowly. We shorten that gap so geological, production and safety information reaches planning while it is still current.

Data we work with
Drilling & borehole records Geotechnical logging Production, grade & recovery Fleet & plant utilisation HSE & environmental monitoring Shift & crew productivity
Typical applications
  • Geotechnical data management and reporting
  • Production and recovery dashboards
  • Equipment downtime and cost analysis
  • Regulatory and compliance reporting
02 Plants & production lines

Industrial & Manufacturing

Plants run on process data that is often trapped inside the control system. We surface it in a form production managers, quality teams and finance can all use — connecting throughput, downtime, quality and cost in one picture.

Data we work with
SCADA, PLC & historian data Line throughput & yield Quality, scrap & rework Maintenance & breakdown logs Energy consumption Batch & traceability records
Typical applications
  • OEE and line performance dashboards
  • Downtime cause and cost analysis
  • Quality trend and exception reporting
  • Shift and plant benchmarking
03 Generation & networks

Energy & Utilities

Generation, distribution, water and waste networks are measured densely and continuously. The challenge is rarely a shortage of readings — it is turning them into early warning, load insight and defensible regulatory reporting.

Data we work with
Metering & consumption Generation & load telemetry Network condition monitoring Water quality & flow Outage & fault records Response & restoration times
Typical applications
  • Network and asset condition monitoring
  • Consumption and loss analysis
  • Outage response and reliability reporting
  • Environmental compliance reporting
04 Projects & delivery

Infrastructure & Construction

Projects are judged on programme, cost and quality, yet the evidence for all three is usually spread across site diaries, spreadsheets and email. We consolidate it into a project record that supports both delivery and dispute.

Data we work with
Geotechnical & materials testing Progress & programme records Quantities & measurement Plant, labour & subcontractors Site safety & inspections Variations & instructions
Typical applications
  • Project progress and cost dashboards
  • Geotechnical investigation reporting
  • Quality assurance and test tracking
  • Site safety and compliance oversight
05 Farming & agri-processing

Agriculture

Farming and agri-processing run on narrow margins, short seasonal windows and biological variability. The data already exists — in agronomy records, machinery telemetry, irrigation systems and packhouse scans — but rarely in one place, and rarely in time for the decision it should be informing.

Data we work with
Yield & block-level production Soil, moisture & weather Irrigation & water use Input application records Machinery utilisation Packhouse & cold chain
Typical applications
  • Yield versus input cost analysis by block
  • Irrigation and water-use efficiency reporting
  • Machinery utilisation and downtime tracking
  • Cold chain monitoring and traceability
06 Facilities & laboratories

Health

Hospitals, clinics, laboratories and medical supply chains operate under tight capacity, strict regulation and unusually sensitive data. Operational analytics can materially improve patient flow, equipment availability and quality reporting — provided privacy and clinical governance are designed in from the outset rather than retrofitted.

Data we work with
Patient flow & bed occupancy Theatre & clinic utilisation Equipment availability Stock & cold chain Laboratory turnaround times Quality & incident indicators
Typical applications
  • Patient flow and capacity dashboards
  • Theatre and clinic utilisation reporting
  • Equipment uptime and maintenance analytics
  • Quality and regulatory reporting

On sensitive data: health information is special personal information under POPIA. We work with de-identified or aggregated data wherever the operational question allows, and handle identifiable records only under a written operator agreement — see our Data Protection Notice.
