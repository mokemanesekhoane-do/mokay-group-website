Where We Specialise

Five Sectors Where Operational Data Carries Real Consequence

We do not serve every industry. We work where asset performance, safety, productivity and real-time data have direct commercial and human impact — and where the cost of deciding late is measurable.

01 — Mining & Resources

Mining & Resources

From exploration drilling through extraction, processing and rehabilitation, mining generates data continuously and consumes it slowly. We shorten that gap so geological, production and safety information reaches planning while it is still current.

Data we work with
  • Drilling, borehole and geotechnical records
  • Production, grade and recovery figures
  • Fleet and plant utilisation
  • HSE and environmental monitoring
Typical applications
  • Geotechnical data management and reporting
  • Production and recovery dashboards
  • Equipment downtime and cost analysis
  • Regulatory and compliance reporting
02 — Industrial & Manufacturing

Industrial & Manufacturing

Plants run on process data that is often trapped inside the control system. We surface it in a form production managers, quality teams and finance can all use — connecting throughput, downtime, quality and cost in one picture.

Data we work with
  • SCADA, PLC and process historian data
  • Line throughput and yield
  • Quality, scrap and rework records
  • Maintenance and breakdown logs
Typical applications
  • OEE and line performance dashboards
  • Downtime cause and cost analysis
  • Quality trend and exception reporting
  • Shift and plant benchmarking
03 — Energy & Utilities

Energy & Utilities

Generation, distribution, water and waste networks are measured densely and continuously. The challenge is rarely a shortage of readings — it is turning them into early warning, load insight and defensible regulatory reporting.

Data we work with
  • Metering and consumption data
  • Generation, load and network telemetry
  • Water quality and flow monitoring
  • Outage, fault and response records
Typical applications
  • Network and asset condition monitoring
  • Consumption and loss analysis
  • Outage response and reliability reporting
  • Environmental compliance reporting
04 — Infrastructure & Construction

Infrastructure & Construction

Projects are judged on programme, cost and quality, yet the evidence for all three is usually spread across site diaries, spreadsheets and email. We consolidate it into a project record that supports both delivery and dispute.

Data we work with
  • Geotechnical and materials testing
  • Progress, programme and quantity records
  • Plant, labour and subcontractor utilisation
  • Site safety and inspection records
Typical applications
  • Project progress and cost dashboards
  • Geotechnical investigation reporting
  • Quality assurance and test tracking
  • Site safety and compliance oversight
05 — Logistics & Transport

Logistics & Transport

Fleets and distribution networks produce location, movement and throughput data constantly. Used well it exposes where cost, delay and risk actually sit — rather than where they are assumed to sit.

Data we work with
  • Telematics, GPS and route data
  • Fuel, maintenance and fleet cost
  • Load, throughput and turnaround times
  • Driver behaviour and incident records
Typical applications
  • Fleet utilisation and cost-per-kilometre
  • Turnaround and bottleneck analysis
  • Route and scheduling performance
  • Driver safety and risk reporting
Why These Five

Different sectors, but the same underlying problem — abundant data, scarce insight, and decisions that cannot wait for month-end.
