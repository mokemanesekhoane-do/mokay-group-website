In Final Testing

Geotechnical Drilling Data System

An integrated management system for geotechnical drilling operations, built to centralize field data capture, real-time project monitoring and automated report generation in a single environment — designed for the operational realities of mine site drilling programmes.

  • Drilling management Planning, scheduling and tracking from initial drilling through completion, with stratigraphy documentation and site mapping.
  • Field data capture Purpose-built forms supporting SPT, Shelby, UDS and in-situ testing across multiple device types.
  • Sample & test management Tracking through to completion with chain-of-custody records and automated calculations.
  • Real-time reporting Automated client-ready geotechnical reports from live project data.
  • Analytics dashboards Core recovery rates, trend analysis and performance indicators at a glance.
  • Role-based access Differentiated permissions for administrators, field teams and clients.

SiteSafety

Real-Time HSE Data Analytics System

A real-time health, safety and environmental data analytics system built to help mining and industrial operations align with HSE regulatory requirements. Safety and environmental data is captured and processed as it is generated, giving operational and compliance teams immediate visibility into HSE performance.

  • Real-time capture Safety and environmental data captured across the operation as it is generated.
  • Immediate visibility Live HSE performance for operational and compliance teams, not a monthly retrospective.
  • Regulatory alignment Supported through a proactive, evidence-based approach to HSE requirements.
  • Audit-ready records Structured to the governance requirements your reporting has to satisfy.
