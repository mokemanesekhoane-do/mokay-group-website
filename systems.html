Geotechnical Drilling Data System

01 The Operational Challenge

Drilling programmes generate a great deal of valuable data and lose much of its value in transit. Field records are captured on paper or loose spreadsheets, samples are tracked informally, and the report a client eventually receives is assembled by hand weeks after the rig has moved on. Errors are found late, chain of custody is difficult to prove, and planning teams end up making decisions on information they cannot fully verify.

02 The Solution We Developed

GeoCorelytics is an integrated management system for geotechnical drilling operations. It brings borehole planning, structured field capture, sample and test tracking, live analytics and automated report generation into a single environment — so data is recorded once, validated at the point of capture, and available to everyone who needs it from that moment on.

03 How The System Works
PlanBoreholes are planned and scheduled, with targets and locations mapped before mobilisation.
CaptureField teams record on purpose-built forms, on device, with validation as they type.
TrackSamples and tests move through the workflow with chain-of-custody recorded at each hand-off.
ReportDashboards update live and client-ready reports generate from the same verified data.
04 Key Features
  • Drilling management Planning, scheduling and tracking from first metre through completion, with stratigraphy documentation and site mapping.
  • Field data capture Purpose-built forms supporting SPT, Shelby, UDS and in-situ testing across multiple device types.
  • Sample & test management Tracking through to completion with chain-of-custody records and automated calculations.
  • Real-time reporting Client-ready geotechnical reports generated automatically from live project data.
  • Analytics dashboards Core recovery rates, trend analysis and performance indicators visible at a glance.
  • Role-based access Differentiated permissions for administrators, field teams and clients.

SiteSafety

Real-Time HSE Data Analytics System

01 The Operational Challenge

Most operations collect safety and environmental data conscientiously and analyse it far too late. Inspections sit in books, observations are logged in spreadsheets, and the first consolidated view arrives in a monthly report — by which time a developing pattern has had four weeks to become an incident. When a regulator or auditor asks for evidence, assembling it is a project in itself.

02 The Solution We Developed

SiteSafety captures health, safety and environmental data as it is generated and processes it immediately. Operational and compliance teams see current HSE performance rather than a retrospective, critical controls are verified and tracked, and the underlying record is structured from the outset to satisfy audit and regulatory scrutiny.

03 How The System Works
CaptureInspections, observations and readings are logged on device at the point they happen.
VerifyCritical controls are checked against requirement, with exceptions raised immediately.
AnalyseTrends, repeat findings and leading indicators surface as the data arrives.
EvidenceActions are tracked to closure and the audit record assembles itself.
04 Key Features
  • Real-time capture Safety and environmental data recorded across the operation as it is generated.
  • Control verification Critical controls checked, dated and attributed, with gaps escalated automatically.
  • Leading indicators Near-miss and hazard trends surfaced early, rather than after a lost-time injury.
  • Audit-ready records Structured to the governance and regulatory requirements your reporting must satisfy.
