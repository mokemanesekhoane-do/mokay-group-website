Who We Are

A Specialist Data Analytics And Operational Intelligence Company

Mokay Group was established to close a gap we kept encountering: operationally critical organisations were generating enormous volumes of data, and converting almost none of it into decisions they could act on in time.

The tools available were usually one of two things — too generic to reflect how a plant, mine or fleet actually runs, or too complex for the operational teams expected to use them every shift. Neither produced the outcome the business needed.

We built a more disciplined alternative. We design, build and support analytics platforms, dashboards and data pipelines engineered specifically for environments where uptime, safety and asset performance carry real consequence — developed in consultation with the people who use them.

  • Six specialist sectors, seven core capabilities
  • Two proprietary operational platforms

How We Handle Data

Your Operational Data Stays Yours

We work with information that is commercially sensitive and, in safety and personnel records, often personal. We treat both accordingly.

  • Personal information processed in line with POPIA
  • Access controlled by role, with activity logged
