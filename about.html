Purpose-Built Expertise

A Defined Gap, Not A General Market

Mokay Group of Companies was established to address a defined gap in the mining and industrial sector: operations were accumulating substantial volumes of data, without the systems in place to convert that data into reliable, actionable insight.

Available solutions were frequently either too generalized for the operational demands of a mine or plant, or too complex for practical use by operational teams.

Mokay Group of Companies was formed to deliver a more disciplined alternative — data analysis systems designed specifically for industrial and mining environments, developed in consultation with the operational personnel who use them.

  • Established 2023, headquartered in Pretoria East
  • Serving mining and industrial operations across South Africa
  • Two core systems in final development and testing
